Version 1: Brand / manufacturer buyer outreach

Goal: identify active buyers for ingredients, packaging components, and merchandise, then convert to qualified demand with clear specs and timelines.

Buyer qualification checklist

  1. What are they sourcing right now (ingredient, packaging, merchandise SKU)?
  2. Spec requirements: grade, mesh, dimensions, materials, compliance requirements.
  3. Quantity and cadence: trial, monthly run-rate, annual band.
  4. Timeline: next PO date and urgency.
  5. Commercial guardrails: target price range, region, incoterms.
  6. Documentation requirements: COA, GMP, allergen, testing, artwork compliance.

Email script — buyer cold outreach

Subject: sourcing support for [ingredient/packaging/merchandise] at [Company]

Hi [First],

I’m [Your name] with [Marketplace]. We help brands and manufacturers source vetted supply across ingredients, packaging, and branded merchandise with clear specs, MOQ, and documentation.

Are you the right contact for procurement or raw materials at [Company]? If yes, I can share matched options based on your current requirements.

Thanks,
[Your name]

Follow-up script — buyer

Subject: quick follow-up on sourcing at [Company]

Hi [First],

Following up in case [ingredient/packaging/merchandise] sourcing is active this month. If useful, I can send 2–3 supplier matches aligned to your spec and quantity targets.

[Your name]

Version 2: Supplier outreach

Goal: onboard suppliers with real inventory and complete documentation, then route matched demand while protecting identities until buyer commitment.

Supplier qualification checklist

  1. What SKUs are available now (ingredient lots, packaging runs, merchandise stock)?
  2. MOQ, lead time, location, shipping lanes.
  3. COA and supporting docs availability (required for listings).
  4. Pricing structure and validity window.
  5. Near-expiry or overstock status and urgency.
  6. Commercial terms: payment terms, sample policy, refund/dispute process.

Deal handoff and close rules

  • Supplier listing must include COA reference before active matching.
  • Supplier identity stays hidden until buyer commits and accepts terms.
  • Deal is marked closed only after commercial confirmation and logged gross value.
  • Brokerage (e.g. 5% on closed deals) and supplier subscription billing are captured per platform terms.
