{{ site_name }} is a private B2B marketplace for the sports nutrition industry, connecting qualified buyers — brands, manufacturers, and distributors — with vetted, onboarded ingredient suppliers.

What we do

We give suppliers a professional storefront for bulk ingredients, flavorings, and packaging, and give buyers a single place to source verified inventory. Every supplier is onboarded personally by our team, and every lot must carry a Certificate of Analysis, specification sheet, and full documentation packet before it goes live.

How sourcing works

Buyers inquire on any listing. Our team confirms specifications, quotes volume pricing, and coordinates terms. Large bulk orders are typically settled by invoice, wire, or ACH after both sides agree — the same way the ingredient trade already works, with the convenience of an online catalog.

Live auctions

For overstock, clearance, and time-sensitive lots, suppliers can run live auctions so competitive bidding moves volume quickly. Supplier identity stays protected behind a masked supplier code until commitment.
