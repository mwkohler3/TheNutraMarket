1) Supplier exclusivity term sheet (call-ready)

Use this in first commercial calls before circulating the full MSA.

SUPPLIER EXCLUSIVITY TERM SHEET (DRAFT)

1. Parties. The parties to this term sheet are [Supplier Legal Name] ("Supplier") and [Your Legal Name] ("Company").

2. Covered Products. Covered Products shall mean the specific ingredient and/or flavor system SKUs identified by name, specification, and grade in the final commercial schedule.

3. Territory and Channels. The exclusivity scope shall apply only within the agreed Territory and Channels, with any explicit exclusions stated in writing.

4. Exclusivity Grant. Supplier shall appoint Company as its exclusive [distributor/broker] for the Covered Products in the agreed Territory and Channels during the term, subject to performance conditions in Section 6.

5. Term. The initial term shall be [12/24/36 months], with renewal mechanics defined by written renewal terms or automatic extension as negotiated.

6. Performance Conditions. Exclusivity shall be conditioned on minimum commercial performance, including annual volume and quarterly run-rate targets. A cure period of [30-60 days] shall apply before any downgrade of exclusivity.

7. Economics. Commercial economics shall include price mechanism, payment terms, rebates or commissions, and freight/incoterm allocation, all to be set in the commercial schedule.

8. Supply and Forecasting. Company shall provide rolling forecasts, and Supplier shall fulfill accepted orders in accordance with agreed lead times and allocation priority.

9. Quality and Compliance. Supplier shall provide required COAs and maintain product conformity to agreed specifications, while complying with applicable regulatory and claims requirements.

10. Non-Circumvention. Supplier shall not bypass Company for introduced or covered accounts in the Territory during the term and for a post-term protection period of [12-24 months].

11. Customer Ownership. Ownership of introduced accounts shall be determined by documented CRM evidence and first qualified introduction date, with tail rights defined in the commercial schedule.

12. Termination. The final contract shall include termination for cause with cure rights, optional termination for convenience if agreed, and defined treatment of inventory and in-flight purchase orders.

13. Next Step. Upon signature of this term sheet, the parties shall finalize and execute a Master Supplier Exclusivity Agreement and Commercial Schedule (Exhibit A).

2) Master supplier exclusivity agreement (MSA draft)

MASTER SUPPLIER EXCLUSIVITY AGREEMENT (DRAFT)

This Master Supplier Exclusivity Agreement (the "Agreement") is made effective as of [Date] by and between [Supplier Legal Name], a [Jurisdiction] [Entity Type] ("Supplier"), and [Your Legal Name], a [Jurisdiction] [Entity Type] ("Company").

1. Definitions. "Covered Products" means the products listed in each executed Commercial Schedule. "Territory" means the geography listed in each Commercial Schedule. "Channels" means the market channels listed in each Commercial Schedule. "Introduced Account" means an account first documented in Company's CRM and accepted as qualified in writing by Supplier, or otherwise evidenced by first substantive account introduction by Company.

2. Appointment and Exclusivity. Subject to Section 3, Supplier appoints Company as its exclusive [distributor/broker] for Covered Products in the Territory and Channels stated in each Commercial Schedule. Supplier shall not, directly or indirectly, sell Covered Products in conflict with Company's exclusivity rights during the applicable term.

3. Performance Conditions. Exclusivity is conditioned on Company meeting minimum performance metrics set in the Commercial Schedule, including purchase volume, run-rate, or revenue thresholds. If Company fails to meet such metrics, Supplier shall provide written notice and a cure period of [30-60] days, or such other period set in the applicable Commercial Schedule. If uncured after the cure period, Supplier may convert rights to non-exclusive status or adjust scope of territory.

4. Forecasting, Orders, and Supply. Company shall provide rolling non-binding forecasts as stated in the Commercial Schedule. Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to fulfill accepted orders according to agreed lead times and shall promptly notify Company of any shortage, delay, allocation event, or material supply risk.

5. Pricing and Payment. Pricing, rebates or commissions, and payment terms are set forth in each Commercial Schedule. Except as otherwise stated in a Commercial Schedule, prices exclude taxes, duties, and freight.

6. Quality, Safety, and Compliance. Supplier warrants that Covered Products conform to agreed specifications and COA requirements. Supplier shall maintain required manufacturing and quality standards and provide documentation reasonably requested by Company. Supplier shall provide prior written notice of any material change to formulation, manufacturing site, testing methods, or specifications. Neither party shall make unauthorized regulatory, medical, or efficacy claims.

7. Intellectual Property and Marketing. Each party retains ownership of its intellectual property. Company may use Supplier trademarks and approved product claims solely as permitted in writing by Supplier. Marketing copy referencing Supplier shall follow the approval process set by the parties.

8. Non-Circumvention and Account Protection. Supplier shall not circumvent Company to transact Covered Products with Introduced Accounts in the Territory and Channels during the term and for [12-24] months thereafter. In the event of breach, Company shall be entitled to the agreed economic remedy set forth in the Commercial Schedule and all available legal and equitable relief.

9. Confidentiality. Each party shall keep non-public commercial and technical information confidential and use such information only as necessary to perform obligations under this Agreement.

10. Warranties and Indemnities. Supplier shall indemnify Company against third-party claims arising from product defects, non-conformance, or Supplier's legal or regulatory non-compliance. Company shall indemnify Supplier for third-party claims arising from Company's unauthorized marketing or claims conduct.

11. Limitation of Liability. Except for confidentiality breaches, IP infringement, indemnity obligations, fraud, or willful misconduct, neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Aggregate liability cap shall be [insert multiple of fees/annual value] per claim period.

12. Term and Termination. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with this Section. Either party may terminate for material breach after written notice and expiration of cure period. On termination, rights under active Commercial Schedules shall end per those schedules, and sell-off plus in-flight order treatment shall follow applicable Commercial Schedule terms.

13. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution. Governing law shall be [State/Country]. Disputes shall be resolved in [court/arbitration body], except either party may seek injunctive relief for exclusivity or confidentiality breaches.

14. Miscellaneous. This Agreement, together with all executed Commercial Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. In case of conflict, Commercial Schedule commercial terms control, then this Agreement. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.

15. Signatures. The parties execute this Agreement through their duly authorized representatives as set out below.

3) Exhibit A — commercial schedule template

COMMERCIAL SCHEDULE (EXHIBIT A) — [SUPPLIER] x [YOUR COMPANY]

1. Covered Products. The covered SKUs, product names, specification details, and packaging configurations shall be set out in this section.

2. Territory and Channels. This section shall define territory, included channels, and any express exclusions.

3. Commercial Terms. This section shall define base price methodology, volume tiers, payment terms, incoterms, and currency treatment.

4. Performance Metrics. This section shall define annual minimums, quarterly milestones, ramp periods, and cure periods.

5. Supply Terms. This section shall define forecast cadence, lead time commitments, MOQ, and allocation or safety stock commitments.

6. Quality and Documentation. This section shall define COA requirements, testing scope, and change-control notice obligations.

7. Account Protection. This section shall define introduced account procedures, tail periods, and non-circumvention remedies.

8. Term and Renewal. This section shall define effective date, initial term, and renewal mechanics.

9. Notes and Deviations. This section shall capture negotiated exceptions, approvals, and side-letter references.
