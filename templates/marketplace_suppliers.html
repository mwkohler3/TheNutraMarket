Listing requirements

What documentation is required?

Every lot must include a COA reference (file name or document ID) before it can go live. Specification sheets, allergen statements, and origin documentation strengthen buyer confidence and speed approvals.

What product categories can I list?

Ingredients and actives, flavorings, and packaging materials. Overstock, clearance, and near-expiry lots are welcome when documentation is current.

Shop now vs. live auction — which should I use?

Shop now is for flat-rate lots at a set price per kg. Live auction is for overstock or time-sensitive inventory where competitive bidding helps you move volume quickly.

Can I list without team approval?

No. All suppliers are onboarded personally. Submit the request form above and our team will reach out to complete setup before your first lot is published.

How are commercial terms handled?

{{ site_name }} facilitates introductions and inventory visibility only. Pricing, payment, shipping, and contracts are negotiated directly between you and the buyer.
